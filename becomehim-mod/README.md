# Become HIM...

Turn into the FroggyDude: gallop on all fours, use his tongue, go into phase 2, rip, eat and hunt — with your own
skin. Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47.4.10+, needs **GeckoLib 4.4.9+**. Works in multiplayer (everyone with the mod sees
everyone's FroggyDude; players without it see a normal player).

Vire o FroggyDude: galope de quatro, a língua dele, a fase 2, arrancar, comer e caçar — com a sua própria skin.
Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47.4.10+, precisa do **GeckoLib 4.4.9+**. Funciona no multiplayer.

## Controls / Controles

- **R** or **double-tap sneak** opens the wheel / **R** ou **dois toques no agachar** abrem a roda (no celular, os
  dois toques funcionam com qualquer botão).
  - **Center**: Human (turns it off) / Humano (desliga).
  - **Inner ring**: Gallop on 4, Standing, Phase 2 / Galope de 4, Em pé, Fase 2.
  - **Outer ring (actions / ações)**: neck crack, rip the arm, rip the head, steal the weapon, ceiling, rage "()",
    tired, tongue whip, tongue pull / estalar o pescoço, arrancar o braço, arrancar a cabeça, roubar a arma, teto,
    raiva "()", cansado, chicote e puxão da língua. Moves marked as *armed* fire on your next attack.
  - **Cameras / câmeras**: normal, behind-low, side, face, victim.
- **V**: next camera / próxima câmera.
- Attack while galloping: pounce (far) or the **tongue** (close). Standing: hammer (close) or the tongue (3-8 blocks).
  Holding someone on the tongue: attack again = pull and bite, jump = throw.
  Atacar galopando: bote (longe) ou a **língua** (perto). Em pé: marreta (perto) ou a língua (3 a 8 blocos).
  Segurando na língua: atacar de novo = puxa e morde, pular = arremessa.
- Hold sneak while galloping to charge the sky jump / segurar o agachar no galope carrega o pulo do céu.

## Gameplay notes / Avisos

- Losing an arm is real: -3 hearts, no offhand, bleeding, until you die. Rip the head: the victim can break free (1
  shot, 2 weapon hits or 3 punches) or dies. Perder o braço é de verdade até morrer; na cabeça dá pra se soltar.
- No FroggyDude voice or sounds are included: only Minecraft sounds. / Sem a voz nem sons do FroggyDude.
